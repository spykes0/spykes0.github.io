ytfarm

A private tool that uploads and schedules videos on its owner's own YouTube channels.

ytfarm produces videos for a small group of YouTube channels and publishes them at planned times. It uses YouTube API Services to upload each finished video, set its thumbnail and playlist, and schedule its release.

ytfarm is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no other users and no accounts besides its owner's. It only acts on channels its owner has connected with Google's own sign-in, and only after the owner approves each upload.

How your data is handled

Read the privacy policy. In short: ytfarm keeps only what it needs to upload to the owner's channels, stores the sign-in encrypted, and never sells or shares data.
