Privacy policy

ytfarm · Effective 28 October 2026

ytfarm is a private tool that uploads and schedules videos on its owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no users besides its owner. This policy explains what ytfarm does with data it gets through YouTube API Services.

YouTube API Services

ytfarm uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel to ytfarm, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What ytfarm accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google's own sign-in, ytfarm accesses:

  • the connected channel's ID, title and handle, to confirm it is uploading to the right channel;
  • the channel's own uploads and their privacy status and scheduled release time, to find free release slots;
  • the channel's playlists, to add new videos to the right one.

It uses that permission to upload the owner's videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists, and schedule their release. It does not read comments, messages, subscribers, viewers or any other person's data.

What ytfarm stores

  • The sign-in: a Google refresh token for each connected channel, encrypted, in a private repository that only the owner can access.
  • Upload records: the IDs, titles, links and scheduled times of the videos ytfarm uploaded.

ytfarm has no website accounts, sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, and collects nothing from people who visit these pages.

Sharing

ytfarm does not sell, rent or share data with anyone. Data goes only to Google, through YouTube API Services, to carry out the uploads the owner approved.

Keeping and deleting data

The sign-in is kept while a channel stays connected. To cut ytfarm off, remove its access in your Google account's security settings. From then on, the stored sign-in no longer works, and it is deleted when the channel is disconnected from ytfarm. Upload records are kept as the owner's own log of their channel and are deleted on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and its date changes with it.
